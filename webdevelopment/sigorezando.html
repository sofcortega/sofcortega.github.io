Sigo Rezando (2024)

Acrylic ink screen printed onto discarded 17"x15" silkscreen frame woven back together with 10/2 cotton yarn. The imagery presented is of my grandmother’s first communion in Venezuela and serves to weave together something that has lost its use (a silkscreen frame with the screen cut out). I’m making an alternative idol of worship out of my grandmother who is a devout Catholic, and creating an alternate meaning to the devotion in my life
