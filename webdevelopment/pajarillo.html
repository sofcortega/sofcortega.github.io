Pajarillo (2024)

This projection mapped piece was created for the 2024 Digital Graffiti Festival in Alys Beach featuring imagery of Venezuelan bolivares, Cattleya orchids, and Guacamaya wings. The imagery and soundscape featured here were an ode to my childhood and experience having roots in one country while growing up in another. This was mapped with three 10,000 lumen projectors and created entirely in Adobe After Effects.
